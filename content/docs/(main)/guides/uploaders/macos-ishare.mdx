---
title: 'macOS: ishare'
description: Use iSshare to upload screenshots/recordings on macOS
---

Zipline can generate a `.iscu` (iShare Custom Uploader) file for you to use with [iShare](https://github.com/castdrian/ishare). With this you can easily upload screenshots/recordings on MacOS to your Zipline instance using iShare.

## Downloading

1. Head to your settings page (click your user icon in the top right corner)

![manage account](/img/docs/uploader-manage-account.png)

2. Scroll down to the "Generate Uploaders" section and click "ishare"

![generate uploaders](/img/docs/generate-uploaders-section.png)

3. Feel free to customize the options provided, then at the bottom you click "Download" to download the `.iscu` file.

![customize options](/img/docs/ishare-1.png)

## Importing

4. Follow the steps below to import the `.iscu` file into ishare.

![open uploaders](/img/docs/ishare-2.png 'Open ishare through the menu bar and click on "Uploaders" then "Import"')

![downloaded file](/img/docs/ishare-3.png 'Drag the downloaded .iscu file into ishare after clicking the "Import" button in Uploader settings')

![see uploader](/img/docs/ishare-4.png 'You should now see the upload in the list of uploaders. Additionally, you can click on the cloud icon to test the uploader.')

![set as default](/img/docs/ishare-5.png 'Click on the ishare app in the menu bar and then set the upload destination as Zipline')
